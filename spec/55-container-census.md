# §55 — The container census: physical, linked, and dark

Three numbers before a container's member list, answering whether it is a real place, a
reading, or a graveyard. PHYSICAL — residents of the container's own attached store. LINKED —
deltas the primary holds and this container gathers, by membership or as a composed pool's
copy. DARK — surviving data members no registered reading can show anyone: the read-side soup
detector, §49.5's complement. Settled and built 2026-08-29, in one conversation.

## 55.1 Physical and linked fall out of the posture model

A separate-posture container (and every inbox pool) holds residents in its own attached
store — physical. A shared-posture container holds nothing; its membership selects
primary-ground deltas — linked. A parent whose gather composes inbox pools reports each
pool's contribution apart — physical elsewhere, named by pool — and a delta the primary holds
is always the primary's, however many pools carry a seeded copy (the promote button's own
rule). Attribution intersects only against pools the gather actually composed: never a
detached pool, never an attachment the process holds without its quarantine registration.

## 55.2 Two levels, deliberately, and labeled

The physical/linked/elsewhere buckets count the GROUND — every byte the scope composes,
struck and striking alike, because "how many deltas are here" asks about bytes. The lit/dark
split counts only SURVIVING data members — readability asks what a reader can still see, and
a struck stray alarming dark forever would be the over-alarm this metric forbids. The scope's
negation closure carries every strike beside its target, so survival is decided from the
members alone, closed over exactly the grounds composed. Strikes class law and land in the
vocabulary bucket with the rest of the constitution — never counted as soup.

## 55.3 Dark is decided by the surviving-lens union

The predicate is the listing door's own: a surviving data member is LIT when any of its
entity-pointer contexts — or any of its pointer roles, because a byRole-grouped body files
under roles — appears in a surviving program's union (`survivingContextsOf`, read from the
grouping and never the flat list, so a superseded binding's contexts light nothing and two
coexisting sibling lenses both keep theirs). Dark is everything else, and it is an
UNDERCOUNT by design: a reading's own term can filter a delta whose context matches, and an
advisory number must never over-alarm. The one residue is stated where it lives: a
{const}-grouped body's members are not identifiable from their own pointers, and no shape
Loam mints groups that way.

## 55.4 The surface

The container detail page renders the census above the member list — physical, linked, each
pool's elsewhere line, dark with the safe-direction sentence in its own words, and the
vocabulary bucket — from the scope read that page already pays. The dashboard tree stays
census-free: annotating every node eagerly would multiply the attention panel's stated bill
(§49.3), and the swept-index follow-on is where that becomes affordable.

## What this section refuses

No store-total dedup in v1, no dashboard-tree annotation, no storage mutation of any kind:
the census is a reading over deltas that already exist, and its numbers exist to drive
register/keep/ticket decisions by a person — never a gate.

Loam is a database you grow.

signed facts · shared ground · apps as lenses

Every fact is a signed, immutable claim. Objects exist because claims point at the same name — nobody creates them, and nobody owns them. Disagreement is data, and resolving it belongs to the reader. An app is a lens over shared ground: push deltas, get software. Your data outlives every app that touches it — and when something must truly be forgotten, it is provably gone, while the ground remembers that it forgot and never what.

whose word

Every line is signed.

Claude writes with a key of its own. The ground records that the connection said it — not you — and the signature travels with the fact forever. Disconnect it and every line stays, still signed as its.

where it lives

Consent names a container.

When you connect Claude you choose where the connection lives — one container under your name. Its writes land in an inbox there; its reads are that container. Nothing else, unless you say so again.

whose law

Readings are yours.

One pile, two readings: the store holds every claim, and a reading decides what they add up to. Two apps — or two friends' stores — can read one ground differently on purpose, with nothing copied.

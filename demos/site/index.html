Every fact is a signed, immutable claim. Objects exist because claims point at the same name — nobody creates them, and nobody owns them. Disagreement is data, and resolving it belongs to the reader. An app is a lens over shared ground: push deltas, get software. Your data outlives every app that touches it — and when something must truly be forgotten, it is provably gone, while the ground remembers that it forgot and never what.
